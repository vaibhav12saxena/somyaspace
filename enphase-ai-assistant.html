Context

Enphase Energy is a global energy technology company that designs and manufactures solar microinverters, battery storage, and EV chargers for residential and commercial markets, and primarily works in B2B2C model.

Enphase provides multiple products for installers. Although each product solves a different part of the installer's job, they operate independently. Installers constantly switch between applications, repeat context, search documentation, and manually connect information across systems.

The Enphase Installer Ecosystem

Business Constraints

Enlighten Manager is a legacy platform used by 100K+ solar professionals globally. Dense information hierarchy, entrenched workflows, and limited frontend flexibility mean we cannot disrupt existing usage patterns. AI assistance must be a separate layer and not embedded within core flows.

Technical Constraints

Installer Toolkit is used by 50K+ field technicians. Commissioning is a 6-step guided process that happens largely offline. Online preparation, then offline commissioning, then online sync. A persistent AI layer is not viable here. AI assistance must be embedded, offline capable, and non-blocking.

User Testing Insights

Finding Action

Installers wanted to set context and filters for every output

Added "restart conversation" and context-switch affordances. Users need to pivot without starting over.

Initial design was extremely conversational

Prioritized chip-based interactions over open text fields. Most installer queries fall into predictable categories.

Morning Brief was opened by 82% of test users on first login

Confirmed the AI Morning Brief as the default landing experience. Proactive summaries beat reactive dashboards.

Installers trusted AI for status checks within the first session

Progressive intelligence validated. Start with confirmations, earn trust, then expand to diagnostics.

Cross-product handoff was the highest-rated feature

Doubled down on the ENLM to Store to ITK context bridge. Made the transition seamless with pre-loaded device recommendations.

Installers wanted chat history and case references

Introduced Chat + Case History feature, bringing memory and continuity into the support experience, grounded in previous interactions.

The question still remained: is this product compatible?

Made compatibility validation a mandatory step in the Store AI flow. This saves returns, RMAs, and installer frustration.

Threaded conversations went unused. Installers preferred a single flowing chat and only referenced history when reviewing past cases.

Removed thread-based navigation entirely. Replaced it with a flat conversation view and a searchable history panel for revisiting older sessions.

Reimagining Enphase Store with AI

Today, the Store knows nothing about the installer's current job. Our research found that installers switch between 3+ apps for a single task, and every purchase starts from a blank search page. With AI, the Store receives full context from ENLM: the site, system size, installed devices, and what's missing. It recommends compatible hardware, flags mismatches, and builds a ready-to-order cart.

In Store, AI is a shopping assistant. It surfaces compatible products, creates bundles, checks inventory, and turns every site activation into a purchasing moment.

Reimagining Installer Toolkit with AI

Commissioning happens offline. A chatbot won't work here. Our research found that 3.2% of all support calls come from installers stuck mid-installation with no guidance. AI is embedded directly into the workflow: a wiring analyzer that catches errors before the installer leaves the roof, and a bulk scanner that replaces 20 minutes of barcode entry with a single photo.

In Toolkit, AI is practical. It validates wiring, bulk-scans devices, pre-caches guidance for offline use, and prevents the support call before it happens.
